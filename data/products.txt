Mokwn provides electronic components and hardware used by engineers,
students, makers, and developers to build technical projects and prototypes.

Main product categories include:

Development Boards:
- ESP32 boards
- Arduino boards
- Microcontroller development boards

Sensors:
- Environmental sensors
- IoT sensors
- NPK soil sensors
- Motion and detection sensors
- Other sensors used in automation and monitoring projects

RFID Components:
- RFID modules
- RFID readers
- RFID-related components used in identification and access-control projects

Electronic Components:
- Components commonly used in embedded systems
- Components for electronics prototypes
- Supporting modules and accessories

Project Tools:
- Tools and hardware used during electronics development and prototyping

These products can be used in projects such as:
- Internet of Things (IoT)
- Embedded systems
- Robotics
- Smart home systems
- Agriculture monitoring
- Automation
- University and graduation projects
- Hardware prototypes

Mokwn focuses on helping builders find the components required to move from
an idea toward a working prototype.
