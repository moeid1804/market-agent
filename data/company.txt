Mokwn is an online electronics components platform designed for engineers,
engineering students, makers, developers, IoT builders, and hardware hobbyists.

Mokwn helps builders move from an idea to a working prototype by providing
electronic components, development boards, sensors, and project-building tools
through one online platform.

The platform is designed around real technical projects rather than only
selling individual components. Typical use cases include IoT systems,
embedded systems, automation, robotics, university projects, and hardware
prototypes.

Mokwn aims to make component discovery and selection easier for builders who
may know what they want to create but do not necessarily know every component
required.

Mokwn also includes Volt, an AI-powered assistant. Users can describe their
project or ask about a component, and Volt helps identify suitable components
that can be added to their cart.

Mokwn's brand slogan is:

"Built for Builders."
