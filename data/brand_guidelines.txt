Mokwn Brand Guidelines

Brand Positioning:
Mokwn is an engineering-focused platform for builders, not simply an online electronics store. The brand should support people who are turning ideas into real technical projects and prototypes.

Brand Slogan:
"Built for Builders."

Target Audience:
- Engineering students
- Makers and hardware hobbyists
- IoT and embedded-system developers
- Engineers
- Early-stage hardware builders

Brand Voice:
Mokwn's communication should be:
- Clear
- Practical
- Friendly
- Technical without being unnecessarily complicated
- Helpful
- Confident
- Builder-focused

Messaging Principles:
Marketing content should focus on solving real builder problems, such as finding suitable components, understanding what is needed for a project, and moving from an idea toward a working prototype.

The brand should communicate value through engineering use cases and project-building experiences rather than focusing only on selling products.

Volt:
Volt is Mokwn's AI-powered assistant. Users can describe a project or ask about a component, and Volt helps them identify suitable components that can be added to their cart.

Volt should be presented as a helpful part of the Mokwn platform, not as the entire Mokwn brand.

Preferred Messaging Themes:
- From idea to prototype
- Build with confidence
- Find the components your project needs
- Make component selection easier
- Support builders throughout the project-building journey

Avoid:
- Unsupported product claims
- Guaranteed project outcomes
- Misleading technical claims
- Overly aggressive sales language
- Presenting Mokwn only as a traditional electronics store
