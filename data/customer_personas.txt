Mokwn.com serves several customer segments with different technical needs.

Persona 1: Engineering Student

Typical profile:
- University student studying engineering or computer science
- Builds course projects, graduation projects, and prototypes
- Interested in Arduino, ESP32, sensors, robotics, and IoT
- Often has limited budget
- May know the project idea but not all required components
- Values clear guidance and easy component selection

Common goals:
- Complete university projects successfully
- Find compatible components quickly
- Learn while building
- Avoid buying unnecessary or incompatible parts


Persona 2: Maker and Hobbyist

Typical profile:
- Enjoys electronics and DIY projects
- Experiments with microcontrollers, sensors, automation, and smart devices
- Frequently builds personal prototypes
- Interested in discovering new components and project ideas

Common goals:
- Explore new technologies
- Build creative projects
- Find components conveniently in one place


Persona 3: IoT and Embedded Developer

Typical profile:
- Developer or engineer building IoT or embedded systems
- Uses development boards, sensors, communication modules, and supporting components
- More technically experienced than beginner users
- Values component specifications, compatibility, and availability

Common goals:
- Build reliable prototypes
- Find suitable components efficiently
- Reduce time spent searching across multiple stores


Persona 4: Early-Stage Hardware Builder

Typical profile:
- Has an idea for a hardware product or prototype
- May understand the desired system but not every required component
- Needs help translating the idea into a practical hardware setup

Common goals:
- Move from idea to prototype
- Identify required components
- Reduce uncertainty during component selection

Volt can support these users by helping them identify suitable components based on their project or technical question.
